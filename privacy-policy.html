Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 9, 2026

Gentoolink Web Services (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you interact with us:

  • Your name, phone number, and email address when you fill out forms (such as Facebook Lead Ads or website forms).
  • Basic business information if you request services (e.g., business name, website, service type).
  • Technical information such as IP address and browser type when you visit our website.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide requested services.
  • Send you visibility reports, proposals, and updates about our services.
  • Improve our website, services, and advertising.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. Information is stored securely and only accessed by authorized personnel.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or delete your information.
  • Withdraw consent to communications at any time.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

Cookies and Tracking

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to improve your experience. You can disable cookies in your browser settings if you prefer.
