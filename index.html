AI Visibility Audit · The Problem

Your Google ranking and your AI visibility are two different things.

A few years ago, ranking on Google was enough. People searched, they clicked, they called.

That's still happening — but something else is happening too.

People are opening ChatGPT and typing "who's the best [plumber / dentist / accountant] near me." They're asking Perplexity for recommendations. They're getting a single AI-generated answer instead of a page of links.

And that AI answer doesn't come from your Google ranking. It comes from a completely different set of signals — structured data, Bing indexing, entity authority, content clarity.

Which means you could be #1 on Google and completely invisible in AI search. At the same time.

The businesses that are showing up didn't get lucky — they're set up correctly.

Free · 60 seconds · No email required

What's your AI visibility score?

Three questions about things you either know or you don't. Your score is worked out from those answers and shown with the arithmetic — nothing gets scanned, nothing gets guessed, and you don't have to give me your email to see it.

1 Is your website indexed by Bing?

Bing is the index behind ChatGPT Search. If Bing doesn't have your site, ChatGPT can't cite it — no matter how well you rank on Google. This is the hardest gate on the list, which is why it's worth the most.

2 Is your Google Business Profile claimed and current?

Hours, categories, and services on a verified profile are among the most reliable facts an assistant can pick up about a local business — and one of the few you control directly.

3 Does your site have structured data describing your business?

JSON-LD markup stating who you are, where you work, and what you sell. It isn't a requirement for appearing in AI answers — Google is clear about that — but it removes the guesswork, and it keeps you eligible for the search features that still exist.

Answer all three to see a score.
