AI Visibility Audit · The Problem

Your Google ranking and your AI visibility are two different things.

A few years ago, ranking on Google was enough. People searched, they clicked, they called.

That's still happening — but something else is happening too.

People are opening ChatGPT and typing "who's the best [plumber / dentist / accountant] near me." They're asking Perplexity for recommendations. They're getting a single AI-generated answer instead of a page of links.

That answer isn't drawn straight from your Google ranking. Some of it is the same groundwork search has always rewarded — but ChatGPT Search reads Bing rather than Google, and an answer that names three businesses has no room for one whose details it can't read with confidence.

Which means you could be #1 on Google and completely invisible in AI search. At the same time.

The businesses that are showing up didn't get lucky — they're set up correctly.

Free · 60 seconds · No email required

What's your AI visibility score?

Three questions about things you either know or you don't. Your score is worked out from those answers and shown with the arithmetic — nothing gets scanned, nothing gets guessed, and you don't have to give me your email to see it.

1 Is your website indexed by Bing?

Bing is the index behind ChatGPT Search. If Bing doesn't have your site, ChatGPT can't cite it — no matter how well you rank on Google. This is the hardest gate on the list, which is why it's worth the most.

2 Is your Google Business Profile claimed and current?

Hours, categories, and services on a verified profile are among the most reliable facts an assistant can pick up about a local business — and one of the few you control directly.

3 Does your site have structured data describing your business?

JSON-LD markup stating who you are, where you work, and what you sell. It isn't a requirement for appearing in AI answers — Google is clear about that — but it removes the guesswork, and it keeps you eligible for the search features that still exist.

Answer all three to see a score.

Common Questions

What we hear before people order.

"Someone else built our site. Can you take it over?"

Usually, yes. Static and standard small business sites move cleanly onto my hosting, and I look before you pay anything. Occasionally a site is tied to a platform that can't be moved without a rebuild — in which case you're at the other door, and I'll say so rather than charge you $400 to find out.

"We barely change anything. Why pay monthly?"

Then Lean is plenty — hosting, backups, and security, with two small edits a quarter, and most of my clients use fewer than that. What you're paying for is that when the hours do change, it's one email and done, not a project. And that the domain, the SSL, and the backups renew on time without you noticing.

"We already have QuickBooks / Jobber / our own system. Aren't we covered?"

Keep it — I'm not selling a replacement. A website and a Google Business Profile sit in front of whatever you already run, and the AI Visibility Audit looks at whether the outside world can find and understand you, not at how your office software works.

"We already do SEO. Isn't that enough?"

Your SEO is working for Google. AI search is a different channel, measured in a different place, and it uses different signals. You can rank #1 on Google and still be invisible when someone asks ChatGPT for what you do in your city.

The audit tells you exactly where the gap is.

"How big is AI search really?"

Big enough that 51% of B2B buyers now start their research in an AI chatbot more often than Google. Big enough that visitors from AI platforms convert at twice the rate of traditional search traffic. And growing fast enough that the businesses optimizing now will have a significant head start over the ones who wait.

"Can't I just do this myself?"

You can — the tools are free. But knowing what to look for, how to implement schema markup correctly, how to restructure content for AI citation, and how to verify it's actually working takes time to learn. Most business owners who try spend months on it and still don't get it right. We do it in 72 hours.
