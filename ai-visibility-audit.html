The Problem

Your Google ranking says nothing about whether AI can find you.

These are two different systems reading two different things. Google ranks pages on backlinks, content relevance and how people behave in the results. ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews work from structured data, Bing's index, whether your business details agree with each other across the web, and whether your pages answer questions in a form a machine can lift.

A business can sit at the top of Google and be completely absent from the AI answer. It happens constantly, and the owner never finds out — there's no ranking report for this, no notification, nothing in Search Console. The first sign is quieter phones with no explanation attached.

According to SOCi's 2026 Local Visibility Index, ChatGPT recommends only 1.2% of local businesses. That number isn't a warning about the future. It's the current hit rate, and most of the businesses in the other 98.8% have never checked.
